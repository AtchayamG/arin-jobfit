# Commercialization & Partner Plan v1

## Standalone offering

The commercially independent value is the job-intelligence layer, not unauthorized access to portal data.

Potential plans:
- Free/local: limited user-supplied JD analysis
- Pro: larger local workspace, CV variants, saved shortlists, interview packs
- Team: shared workspace, policy/admin controls, analytics
- Enterprise: SSO, audit, private deployment, retention controls

Pricing is intentionally not fixed in the blueprint; validate with users first.

## Portal partnership objective

Ask Naukri and Indeed for:
1. confirmation of the appropriate partner path
2. technical/API documentation for the intended job-seeker use, if offered
3. sandbox/test credentials where applicable
4. security/compliance requirements
5. branding/trademark guidance
6. marketplace/distribution options
7. co-innovation, sponsorship, accelerator or commercial partnership possibilities, if available

Do not frame sponsorship as an expectation.

## Partner pitch

Position the products as human-controlled AI interoperability layers that can bring portal-approved workflows into MCP-capable assistants while preserving portal policy, consent and attribution.
